# Understanding Training Data and Models

_Beginner Course · Week 2_

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this week you will be able to:

- Explain the difference between cloud-hosted AI and locally-running AI, including the privacy trade-offs of each
- Define data sovereignty and give a real-world example of why it matters to you personally
- Learn about the importance of open-source models
- Install and run an open-source AI model on your own machine without a cloud account or internet connection
- Train a small custom image classifier using data you collected yourself using the "My Room" in-house app: https://her-ai-studio.github.io/my-room/
- Describe what a model "guardrail" is and explain why someone would add one

---

## What We Will Learn

Last week, we examined some of the inherent problems with the way AI is built, bringing a clear, analytical eye to understanding some of the challenges in this technology. We also learned about counterbalancing some of these challenges by ensuring that diverse minds and experiences are brought to bear when building new technologies, claiming our seat at this table as women builders. Now we will turn our attention to some of the ways that you can become experienced in working with LLMs and other ML models without exposing your data to external systems. Using a web app called "My Room", you will experiment with MobileNet, an image model, installed in the application. Without sharing data, you'll also work with a Ollama, a system that can help you run local LLMs and even work offline while still working with AI.

### Outline

1. **Cloud vs. Local - Pick your System** — The tradeoffs of working online in the cloud, vs. offline locally, and the implications for privacy vs. performance.
2. **Open and Closed - Model Choice** — A walkthrough of the various types of models and their training data
3. **"My Room" - Try a Vision Model** — Categorize your room using a self-hosted model
4. **Moving Offline** — Work with Ollama to download a model and use it offline as a chat interface
